# Jesus-Office

## Approved experience
Edge-to-edge dynamic viewport ancient city inspired by Nazareth/Jerusalem. Zero visible text, including loading, selection, mesh lettering and tooltips. No cards, arrows, roster, telemetry, project catalog or company branding. Nonvisual ARIA names remain. Left drag/one finger pans; right drag/two-finger rotation orbits; wheel/pinch zooms. Keyboard arrows, +/-, 0 reset, Enter selection ring, Escape clear.

## Space and architecture
Real perspective camera with an oblique town overview, 360-degree horizontal orbit and near-top-down to near-ground inspection. No artificial ground-target or zoom-out boundary. View-following ground and adaptive far plane avoid exposing the old rectangular terrain edge. Minimum camera distance and above-ground tilt are deliberate clipping guards. Authored buildings remain finite, not infinitely generated.

Warm limestone, terracotta roofs, timber, olive foliage and restrained patina-teal details. Eight distinct volumetric landmark silhouettes: civic hall, domed bathhouse, watchtower, covered market, amphitheater, aqueduct, pottery kiln and terraced garden/pergola. Actual depth, openings, roof terraces and side/back construction rather than billboard facades. Repeated structural members use instancing. No purchased/generated assets or new dependencies.

## Live-worker core
Seven original stylized visual personas are slots, not a verified registry of named agents. Sanitized real delegated-worker observations drive existing door/lane routes. Preserve open-roof rooms, route clearances, identity stability and complete inactive-pose freeze. No fabricated communication or decorative workers pretending to execute tasks. Architecture and environmental breeze are ambient, not extra agents or task evidence. Laptop bars never display source or secrets.
